MORE THAN TREATMENT

Recovery at Serenity Ranch happens in therapy rooms — and also on walks by the koi pond, in quiet moments of reflection outdoors, and in spaces designed to help the nervous system settle.

Every amenity on the grounds supports rest, reconnection, and the rebuilding of strength.

  • Beautiful Natural Surroundings Designed for Rest & Recovery
