WHOLE-PERSON RECOVERY

Healing the mind. Restoring the body. Renewing the spirit.

At Serenity Ranch, recovery goes beyond symptom management. We treat the whole person through a comprehensive, integrative approach designed specifically for first responders. Our goal is to help you build the foundation for lifelong wellness.

NUTRITION & GUT HEALTH

Proper nutrition plays a vital role in emotional and physical well-being. Residents enjoy chef-prepared meals designed to support healing, energy, and gut health throughout recovery.

PHYSICAL WELLNESS

Exercise, strength training, and movement-based wellness are integrated into recovery to restore physical resilience and support mental clarity.

SOMATIC HEALING

Reconnecting the mind and body is essential for trauma recovery. Somatic approaches help release stored stress and restore regulation.

  • Mindfulness
  • Breathwork
  • Sauna
  • Cold Plunge
  • Recovery-Focused Wellness Activities

SLEEP RESTORATION

Healthy sleep is foundational to healing. Our environment and clinical support help residents improve sleep quality and rebuild restorative rest.

MASSAGE & RECOVERY SERVICES

Therapeutic massage and restorative services support emotional and physical healing, helping the body release tension carried from years of service.

At Serenity Ranch, recovery extends beyond symptom management to help residents build sustainable habits that support lifelong wellness.
