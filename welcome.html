Welcome,

For more than 25 years, I have had the privilege of serving first responders across California, walking beside those who carry the weight of trauma, cumulative stress, and the silent battles that come with a life of service.

Serenity Ranch was created out of that calling: to provide specialized residential treatment designed exclusively for the unique needs of first responders.

Our program brings together clinical treatment, peer support, and medical expertise, delivered by licensed clinicians who understand public safety culture and the realities of the job.

As a private six-bed residential program, we offer the space and individualized attention needed for meaningful healing.

We treat the whole person - mind, body, and spirit - through evidence-based care, nutrition and digestive health, somatic therapies, and wellness services that support lasting recovery.

Thank you for trusting us with this journey. We are honored to serve those who serve others.
