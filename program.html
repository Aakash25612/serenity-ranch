Serenity Ranch provides specialized residential treatment for first responders experiencing Alcohol Use Disorder, Substance Use Disorder, Co-Occurring Mental Health Conditions, trauma, occupational stress injuries, burnout, moral injury, and related behavioral health concerns.

EVIDENCE-BASED TREATMENT

Individual therapy, group counseling, trauma treatment, and medication management tailored to each resident.

PEER SUPPORT INTEGRATED

Connection with others who truly understand your world. Peer support is woven throughout the recovery journey.

PHYSICAL WELLNESS

Fitness, nutrition, outdoor activities, and mindfulness for total health of body, mind, and spirit.

FAMILY RESTORATION

Family involvement and education to heal relationships and build support that lasts.
