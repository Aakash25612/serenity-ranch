Our clinical team is composed of licensed professionals and medical experts who understand the unique culture, stresses, and experiences of first responders. Together, we provide the highest standard of evidence-based, trauma-informed, and holistic care.

JAYSON A. HYMES, M.D., MPH, FAPCM, FASAM, QME

MEDICAL DIRECTOR

Dr. Jayson A. Hymes is triple board-certified in Addiction Medicine, Pain Medicine, and Anesthesiology with over 40 years of experience treating addiction, chronic pain, and complex medical conditions.

A graduate of the University of Louisville School of Medicine and the Harvard School of Public Health, Dr. Hymes completed his residency and fellowship training through Harvard-affiliated teaching hospitals. He is a Fellow of the American Society of Addiction Medicine (FASAM) and a diplomate of the American Board of Addiction Medicine, the American Board of Pain Medicine, and the American Board of Anesthesiology.

As Medical Director, Dr. Hymes provides expert oversight of withdrawal management, complex medical care, and integrated treatment to support optimal outcomes for first responders and high-performance professionals.

SHONNA HILL, PSY.D.

CLINICAL DIRECTOR

Dr. Shonna Hill is a licensed clinical psychologist, former law enforcement officer, and nationally recognized expert in first responder mental health.

With more than 20 years of experience, she has dedicated her career to helping police officers, firefighters, dispatchers, and other public safety professionals recover from trauma, build resilience, and restore well-being.

Dr. Hill has served as lead psychologist on more than 500 critical incidents and is the creator of a law enforcement suicide prevention assessment scale. She has trained agencies nationwide on trauma recovery, peer support, and cultural competence in public safety care.

As Clinical Director of Serenity Ranch First Responder Recovery, Dr. Hill oversees the delivery of individualized, evidence-based, trauma-informed care and maintains the highest standard of clinical excellence throughout the program.

BARBARA PAVLO, PSY.D.

TRAUMA SPECIALIST

Dr. Barbara C. Pavlo is a licensed clinical psychologist with over 25 years of experience as a therapist, educator, consultant, and speaker.

She has served as a police psychologist with the Los Angeles Police Department since 2006, providing clinical care and consulting on high-risk incidents, including SWAT barricades and hostage negotiations.

A recognized expert in police morale, Dr. Pavlo has worked extensively with officers and mental health professionals and co-authored The Blue Morale Project, a key reference in the field. She was awarded the LAPD Meritorious Award for her contributions to officer suicide prevention.

ALEJANDRO ZAVALA, PSY.D., LMFT, CAADC-II

ADDICTION SPECIALIST

Dr. Zavala has over 12 years of experience treating trauma, substance use disorders, and stress-related conditions. His clinical work focuses on first responders and high-risk populations, with expertise in suicide prevention, addiction treatment, and culturally competent care.

He previously served as Director of Behavioral Health Services for UFLAC (United Firefighters of Los Angeles City), where he supported the behavioral health and wellness needs of Los Angeles firefighters and their families.
